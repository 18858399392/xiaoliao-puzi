# Content sources

The text starter entries and candidate catalogue are extracted from the public JSON files in [vikiboss/60s](https://github.com/vikiboss/60s), commit `d582aa65d44f76e91c434174c9cf44684d30755f`. The upstream MIT notice is reproduced in `60s-LICENSE.txt`.

Audio candidates follow the public SingDuck entry pages listed by that upstream module. Each candidate retains its artist, performer, source page and original piece link. The local files are compact MP3 copies of those short public recordings, prepared for the requested local review and starter preview. The upstream software license is not a claim of ownership over the songs or recordings.

The local content review page controls the bundled starter selection. Only explicitly rejected IDs are filtered from subsequent API results; unselected candidates may still appear later from the API.

Online additions use the community [Meting API](https://github.com/xizeyoupan/Meting-API) demo for a NetEase playlist, and the official [Audius read API](https://docs.audius.co/api/). Audius tracks with stream gates or explicitly disabled streaming are excluded. These additions retain the artist and source link and stream on demand; they are not copied into the project's offline audio cache. Some platform URLs provide previews only. API access is not a transfer of copyright; platform and artist restrictions still apply. No VIP, paid, or authentication restrictions are bypassed.
